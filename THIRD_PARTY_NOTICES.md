# Third-party materials

The study evaluates the released CoVT model and Depth Anything V2; it is not an official release by their authors.

- CoVT: https://github.com/Wakals/CoVT . The model and code revisions are recorded in the resource audit. Native operations are reconstructed and checked against that pinned implementation. Full model weights and the downloaded upstream source tree are not redistributed here.
- Depth Anything V2: https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2 . Obtain expert weights separately and follow the terms attached to the selected model variant.
- Figures `docs/research/notes_figures/paper_1.png` through `paper_3.png` reproduce figures from the CoVT paper for attributed research discussion. Copyright remains with the original authors. Provenance is recorded alongside the notes. No new license is granted for these figures.

The remaining experiment code, notes, and result records are maintained as a research snapshot. No blanket open-source license is assigned in this initial private repository. Choose an explicit license and review third-party permissions before a public release.
